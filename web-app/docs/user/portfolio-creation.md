# Portfolio Creation Tutorial

This comprehensive guide walks you through creating your first professional portfolio on Spotlight.

## Before You Start

### What You'll Need
- 10-15 high-quality professional photos
- Your resume or list of experience
- Contact information
- About 20-30 minutes of time

### Photo Preparation Tips
- Select your best 10-15 photos
- Ensure they're high resolution (1920x1080 or higher)
- Include variety: headshots, full body, different outfits/looks
- Make sure file sizes are under 5MB each

## Step-by-Step Portfolio Creation

### Step 1: Access Portfolio Creation

#### From Dashboard
1. Log into your Spotlight account
2. Click **"Create Portfolio"** on your dashboard
3. Or click the **"+"** button in the top navigation

#### Direct Link
Visit `/portfolio/create` after logging in

### Step 2: Basic Information

Fill out your essential details:

#### Personal Information
- **Full Name**: Your professional name as you want it displayed
- **Professional Title**: e.g., "Actor," "Model," "Voice Actor"
- **Location**: City and state/country
- **Age Range**: Optional, but helpful for casting

#### Contact Information
- **Email**: Professional email address
- **Phone**: Mobile number for casting calls
- **Website**: Personal website or social media (optional)
- **Representation**: Agent/manager contact if applicable

#### Bio Section
Write a compelling 2-3 sentence bio:
- Highlight your experience and specialties
- Mention notable work or achievements
- Keep it concise and engaging
- Use active voice

**Example Bio:**
"Experienced stage and screen actor with over 8 years in theater and film. Specializes in dramatic roles and character work, with recent appearances in 'The Crown' and 'Bridgerton.' Fluent in English and Spanish with extensive Shakespeare training."

### Step 3: Template Selection

Choose from our professional templates:

#### Classic Professional
- **Best for**: Traditional headshots and commercial work
- **Style**: Clean, elegant, industry-standard
- **Good for**: All experience levels

#### Modern Bold
- **Best for**: Contemporary looks and fashion work
- **Style**: Striking, colorful, attention-grabbing
- **Good for**: Fashion models, commercial actors

#### Minimal Elegant
- **Best for**: Artistic work and sophisticated brands
- **Style**: Clean lines, lots of white space
- **Good for**: Theater actors, artistic professionals

#### Creative Artistic
- **Best for**: Unique personalities and creative work
- **Style**: Artistic, unconventional layouts
- **Good for**: Character actors, creative professionals

### Step 4: Image Upload and Organization

#### Upload Process
1. Click **"Upload Images"** or drag and drop
2. Select multiple images at once (Ctrl/Cmd + click)
3. Wait for upload completion
4. Images are automatically optimized

#### Image Management
- **Reorder**: Drag and drop to rearrange
- **Primary Image**: Set your best headshot as primary
- **Categories**: Organize by type (headshots, full body, action shots)
- **Alt Text**: Add descriptions for accessibility

#### Image Categories
- **Headshots**: Close-up professional portraits
- **Full Body**: Standing poses showing full figure
- **Action Shots**: Dynamic poses or performance shots
- **Character Work**: Different looks or characters
- **Behind the Scenes**: Optional candid or working shots

### Step 5: Experience and Credits

#### Acting Experience
Add your professional credits:
- **Project Title**: Name of film, show, or production
- **Role**: Character name or description
- **Type**: Film, TV, Theater, Commercial, etc.
- **Year**: When it was produced
- **Director/Company**: Who produced it

#### Skills and Special Abilities
List relevant skills:
- **Languages**: Fluency levels
- **Dialects/Accents**: Regional or character accents
- **Special Skills**: Sports, instruments, dance, etc.
- **Training**: Acting schools, workshops, coaches

#### Education and Training
- **Schools**: Drama schools, universities
- **Coaches**: Notable teachers or mentors
- **Workshops**: Recent classes or intensives
- **Certifications**: Relevant professional certifications

### Step 6: Customization

#### Visual Customization
- **Primary Color**: Choose your brand color
- **Font Selection**: Pick readable, professional fonts
- **Layout Options**: Adjust spacing and arrangement
- **Background**: Select appropriate background styles

#### Advanced Options
- **Custom CSS**: For advanced users (Pro feature)
- **Animation Settings**: Control hover effects and transitions
- **Mobile Layout**: Optimize for mobile viewing

### Step 7: SEO and Discovery

#### SEO Optimization
- **Meta Description**: Brief portfolio description for search engines
- **Keywords**: Relevant industry terms
- **Alt Text**: Descriptive text for all images
- **Custom URL**: Create a memorable portfolio URL

#### Privacy Settings
- **Public**: Visible to everyone and search engines
- **Private**: Only accessible with direct link
- **Password Protected**: Requires password to view
- **Scheduled Publishing**: Set a future publish date

### Step 8: Preview and Test

#### Device Preview
Test your portfolio on:
- **Desktop**: Full layout view
- **Tablet**: Medium screen layout
- **Mobile**: Mobile-optimized view

#### Content Review Checklist
- [ ] All text is error-free
- [ ] Images load quickly and look sharp
- [ ] Contact information is accurate
- [ ] Bio is compelling and professional
- [ ] Experience is up-to-date
- [ ] Navigation works smoothly

#### Performance Check
- [ ] Page loads in under 3 seconds
- [ ] Images are optimized
- [ ] No broken links
- [ ] Mobile experience is smooth

### Step 9: Publishing

#### Final Review
Before publishing:
1. Double-check all information
2. Test on different browsers
3. Have someone else review it
4. Ensure privacy settings are correct

#### Publishing Process
1. Click **"Publish Portfolio"**
2. Confirm your settings
3. Your portfolio goes live immediately
4. You'll receive a confirmation email

#### Post-Publishing
- Share your portfolio URL
- Add it to business cards and resumes
- Include in email signatures
- Post on social media

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Content Mistakes
- **Too Many Images**: Stick to your 10-15 best photos
- **Poor Quality**: Only use professional-quality images
- **Outdated Content**: Remove old or irrelevant work
- **Inconsistent Branding**: Maintain a cohesive look

### Technical Mistakes
- **Large File Sizes**: Optimize images for web
- **Missing Alt Text**: Important for accessibility
- **Broken Links**: Test all external links
- **Mobile Issues**: Always test on mobile devices

### Professional Mistakes
- **Inappropriate Content**: Keep everything professional
- **TMI**: Don't overshare personal information
- **Typos**: Proofread everything carefully
- **Outdated Info**: Keep contact details current

## Troubleshooting

### Upload Issues
- **Large Files**: Compress images before uploading
- **Slow Upload**: Check your internet connection
- **Format Errors**: Convert to JPG or PNG
- **Browser Issues**: Try a different browser

### Display Problems
- **Layout Issues**: Clear browser cache
- **Image Quality**: Check original image resolution
- **Mobile Problems**: Test in different mobile browsers
- **Loading Speed**: Optimize image file sizes

### Publishing Problems
- **Email Verification**: Check your email and verify account
- **Permission Errors**: Ensure you have rights to all content
- **Payment Issues**: Check your subscription status
- **Technical Errors**: Contact support for help
