# Getting Started with Spotlight

Welcome to Spotlight! This guide will help you create your first professional portfolio in just a few minutes.

## What is Spotlight?

Spotlight is a modern portfolio platform designed specifically for actors, models, and creative professionals. Create stunning portfolios that showcase your talent and help you land your next big opportunity.

## Quick Start (5-Minute Setup)

### Step 1: Create Your Account
1. Visit [Spotlight](https://spotlight.com)
2. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your email and create a secure password
4. Verify your email address (check your inbox)

### Step 2: Complete Your Profile
1. Add your basic information (name, profession, location)
2. Upload a professional headshot
3. Write a compelling bio (2-3 sentences about your experience)
4. Add your contact information

### Step 3: Choose a Template
We offer 4 professionally designed templates:
- **Classic Professional** - Traditional and elegant
- **Modern Bold** - Contemporary and eye-catching  
- **Minimal Elegant** - Clean and sophisticated
- **Creative Artistic** - Unique and expressive

### Step 4: Add Your Content
1. **Images**: Upload your best professional photos
2. **Experience**: Add your acting/modeling experience
3. **Skills**: List your special abilities and skills
4. **Contact**: Ensure your contact information is complete

### Step 5: Customize and Publish
1. Customize colors and fonts to match your style
2. Preview your portfolio on different devices
3. Click **"Publish"** when you're ready to go live
4. Share your new portfolio URL with casting directors and agents

## Best Practices

### Profile Photos
- Use high-resolution images (at least 1920x1080)
- Ensure good lighting and professional quality
- Include a variety of shots (headshots, full body, action shots)
- Keep file sizes under 5MB for fast loading

### Writing Your Bio
- Keep it concise (2-3 sentences)
- Highlight your unique selling points
- Include recent notable work or achievements
- Use active voice and confident language

### Portfolio Organization
- Lead with your strongest image
- Group similar types of shots together
- Update regularly with new work
- Remove outdated or lower-quality images

### SEO Optimization
- Use relevant keywords in your bio
- Add alt text to images
- Keep your contact information up to date
- Share your portfolio on social media

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change my template later?
Yes! You can switch templates at any time. Your content will automatically adapt to the new design.

### How do I make my portfolio private?
Go to Settings > Privacy and toggle "Private Portfolio." Only people with the direct link can view it.

### Can I use my own domain?
Yes, Pro users can connect a custom domain (like yourname.com) to their portfolio.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Visit Settings > Billing and click "Cancel Subscription." Your account will remain active until the end of your billing period.

### Is my data secure?
Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures. Read our [Privacy Policy](https://spotlight.com/privacy) for details.
